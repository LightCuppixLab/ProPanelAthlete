# Privacy Policy for Pro Panel Athlete

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Panel Athlete (the "App") handles your data. The App is designed to be **privacy-first** and **on-device only**. It is available exclusively on Apple platforms: iPhone (iOS) and Apple Watch (watchOS). There is no Android version.

## 1. Who We Are

The App is developed by Light Cup Pix Lab, Padova, Italy.

Contact: lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com

## 2. What the App Does With Your Data

**We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server.** There is no account, no sign-in, no backend, and no cloud. The App has no network connection at all: nothing you record or that the App calculates ever leaves your device.

**Health data (read).** With your permission, the App reads data from Apple **HealthKit** to show your daily readiness and training load. On iPhone and Apple Watch it reads: workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), VO₂max, active energy, and sleep. On Apple Watch it additionally reads walking/running distance and cycling distance while you record a workout. All of this is processed **only on your device**.

**Health data (write, Apple Watch only).** If you record a workout with the "Train" feature on Apple Watch, the App saves that workout to Apple Health (the workout session, active energy, and distance). This is the only data the App writes, and it is written only to your own Apple Health, on your device. The App does not write anything to Health on iPhone.

**Information you enter.** You may optionally enter your year of birth, biological sex (used only for the training-load formula), a manually measured maximum heart rate, and your preferred units (metric or imperial). These values are stored **only on your device** and are used only to personalise the calculations. They are never sent anywhere.

**Derived values.** From the data above, the App calculates values such as your readiness score, training load, and daily snapshots. These derived values are kept in a **local database on your device** and can be exported or deleted by you at any time (see "Your Rights and Choices").

**Subscription.** If you purchase the optional "Pro" subscription, your entitlement is verified through Apple StoreKit on your device. We do **not** run any server, we do **not** store a subscription record, and we do **not** receive or store any payment data. See "Payments".

## 3. What the App Does Not Collect

The App does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or camera content, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers. There is **no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting, and no push notifications**. Because nothing is tracked and nothing leaves the device, **no App Tracking Transparency prompt is needed**. We do not receive or store payment card details or any payment instrument data.

## 4. Where Your Data Is Stored

**Everything stays on your device.** Your health data remains managed by Apple Health, and the values the App calculates are stored in a local database on the same device. There is no server, so there is no server-side copy of your data.

The App shares a single value, your current readiness score, with its own Apple Watch complication through a private on-device App Group. This value never leaves your Apple devices.

## 5. Consent

Health data is sensitive. The App reads it only after you grant permission in Apple Health, and it writes a workout only when you choose to record one on Apple Watch. You can grant or revoke this access at any time in the Apple Health app.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal data. Because all data is on your device, you can exercise these choices directly in the App:

- **Access and export.** In the "You" section, use **Export** to obtain a copy of the values the App has calculated.
- **Deletion.** In the "You" section, use **Delete calculated data** to remove the App's stored derived values. Your health data itself stays in Apple Health and is managed by you there.
- **Correction.** Edit your profile settings (units, max heart rate, and so on) directly in the App.

For any request you can contact us at lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

The App keeps the values it calculates on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time in the "You" section, or by deleting the App. Your health data is retained in Apple Health under your control, independently of this App.

## 8. Third Parties

Because nothing leaves your device, the App shares your data with no third party. The only company involved is **Apple**, which processes your subscription purchase and billing through the App Store under its own terms and privacy policy. Apple is not used by the App for advertising or analytics.

## 9. Payments

The App offers an optional paid auto-renewable subscription ("Pro"), available as a monthly plan and a yearly plan; the yearly plan includes an introductory free trial for new subscribers. All purchases are processed and billed entirely by the Apple App Store under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card or any payment instrument data. To unlock Premium features, the App only checks your subscription status locally through Apple StoreKit.

## 10. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your Apple device and by Apple Health's permission system, which lets you grant or revoke the App's access at any time in the Health app. Because the App has no server and no network connection, your data is never exposed in transit.

## 11. Not a Medical Device

Pro Panel Athlete **illustrates your own health and fitness data and calculates a few derived values** (such as readiness and training load). It **does not provide medical, diagnostic, or training advice**, and it is **not a medical device**. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical judgement. Always consult a qualified professional before making decisions about your health or training. Physical activity carries inherent risks, and you are responsible for your own choices.

## 12. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in the App, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

## 14. Contact

For any privacy question or to exercise your choices, contact Light Cup Pix Lab at lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com.
